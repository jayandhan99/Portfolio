Tree Health Classification Using Image Processing

Personal Project

ML

Coconut plantations in regions such as Kerala face severe yield loss from Root Wilt disease, which has no cure once established. Early detection is critical because symptoms often appear in foliage before the disease is visible at ground level.

This project applies computer vision to drone-captured imagery of coconut groves. By analyzing color distributions and pixel-level patterns in canopy images, the pipeline classifies trees as healthy or showing early stress signals associated with Root Wilt and related conditions.

Approach

Images are preprocessed to normalize lighting and focus on canopy regions. Color-space features and texture descriptors capture subtle yellowing and structural changes that precede advanced infection. A supervised classifier is trained on labeled examples to separate healthy palms from those showing early disease indicators.

The goal is operational: flag at-risk trees early so farmers can isolate affected areas, adjust irrigation or nutrition, and limit spread — reducing crop loss and supporting more sustainable plantation management.

Technologies

Python, OpenCV, image processing, and machine learning for aerial crop-health monitoring.
